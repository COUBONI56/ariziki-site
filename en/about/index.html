About

Creating wealth,one product at a time.

ARIZIKI is a SaaS publishing brand. We build tools that give time, clarity and revenue back to the people who use them.

Explore the ecosystem Contact us →

The name

ARIZIKI

means “wealth”.

We chose this name as a compass. To us, wealth is more than a number: it is time given back to teams, more confident decisions, ideas that become useful products. Every ARIZIKI product should create a little more of it for its users.

Our model

A parent brand,focused products.

  1. 01

    One product, one mission

    Each ARIZIKI SaaS focuses on one specific need and solves it thoroughly, rather than doing everything halfway.

  2. 02

    Shared foundations

    Design, infrastructure and security are shared, so every new product starts faster and stronger.

  3. 03

    Continuous improvement

    Our products evolve with their users, guided by their feedback and their results.
